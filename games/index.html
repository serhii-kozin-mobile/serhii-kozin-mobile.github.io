Games

Small, strange games I build in public. They run in the browser on a phone or a desktop, with no install, no account and no ads.

Cozy 3D balance puzzle

Teeter Isles

Build a village on a floating island that tips with every house. Residents who slide off cling to the rim; haul them up, but every rescue jerks the island.

  • 8 islands plus Endless Drift
  • Drag houses in with a live tilt and sway preview
  • Dangle and rescue: tap a name tag to haul a resident back
  • Every island checked by a solver bot and an imprecise-player bot
Phone and desktop · about 20 minutes · no install

Dark occult roguelite

TALLOWBORN

You are a knight of living wax, and your body is the board. Every candle that burns out drips down your body and fuses its power into the candle below.

  • Six sockets on a melting effigy: crown, arms, heart, legs
  • Lean left, upright or right to steer the dripping wax
  • Light is a dial: in Blaze you hit harder, but it sees your heart
  • Lose a limb, gain Fury, then recast it from the wax pool
Phone and desktop · 7 doors · about 15 minutes
Play TALLOWBORN

How they are made

Built with agents, checked by bots

Levels, residents and copy are drafted with AI agents. Every Teeter Isles island is then validated by a look-ahead solver and by a bot that plays like a slightly clumsy human. TALLOWBORN runs are replayed through the real UI in a headless browser before each release.

Are they finished?
Not yet. They are playable prototypes and change often.
Do they track me?
No. Play stats stay in your browser's local storage and never leave it.
